Data-driven solutions for territorial development

My work combines quantitative analysis with participatory methodologies to create evidence-based solutions for territorial development. Here are some featured projects that showcase this approach.

🚀 Data-Driven Solutions

Rural Scholarship Allocation Tool

Organization: Interra Foundation (Colombia) | Period: Sept 2023 - Feb 2024

Created an interactive decision-making tool to optimize scholarship distribution for rural youth, considering territorial variables and socioeconomic data.

Key Impact: - 500+ students benefited from optimized allocation - Reduced analysis time from weeks to hours - Tool permanently adopted by the foundation

Technologies: R, Shiny, optimization algorithms

Family Survey Analysis (EDIFAM)

Organization: IAPF (Mexico) | Period: Aug 2023 - Feb 2024

Statistical analysis of family surveys across 5 Mexican municipalities (San Nicolás de los Garza, Irapuato, San Luis Potosí, Zacatecas, and Tlaxcala).

Key Impact: - Analysis of 5,000+ surveys across 5 municipalities - Reports adopted for municipal policy decisions - Comprehensive analysis of family dynamics and needs

Technologies: R, statistical modeling, survey analysis

🗺️ Territorial Development

National Strategy for Coastal Municipalities

Organization: National Planning Department (Colombia) | Period: Dec 2020 - Sept 2023

Led the design of a comprehensive public policy document for the development of 47 coastal municipalities with high poverty rates.

Key Impact: - Created differentiated strategy for 47 municipalities - Drove institutional building of the National Bioceanic System - Collaboration with Colombian Ocean Commission and World Bank

Technologies: R, QGIS, territorial analysis, participatory methodologies

Local Leaders Identification System

Research Project: Master’s Thesis | Period: 2017 - 2020

Developed an innovative algorithm based on network analysis to systematically identify community leaders in rural areas, addressing coordination problems in closed communities.

Key Impact: - Direct impact on 2,000+ youth across 7 municipalities - Created replicable methodology for leader identification - Published thesis and academic paper

Technologies: R (igraph), network theory, graph centralities

Key Impact: - Direct impact on 2,000+ youth in 7 municipalities (Cundinamarca) - Technology education projects for rural girls - Award for best audiovisual report

Technologies: R (igraph), participatory methodologies, community facilitation
